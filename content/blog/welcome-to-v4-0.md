Personal websites are a special challenge. Within the constraints of a client project, visual presentation sort-of exists with bumpers on either side (at least in my experience). Stray too far in one direction and, inevitably, your course will be redirected toward the center.

You would think removing the bumpers would be all positives from a creative standpoint, but there exists an analysis paralysis that can threaten to stall the project before it even starts. Making a website for yourself forces you to consider, "What do I value?" versus "What does the client value?"

I think this website, Ethan Head V4.0, best exemplifies what I've come to value and strive for as a designer after over a decade in the field more than any previous version. That value is PERSONALITY.

I believe showing your personality is showing authenticity. It's a value I have tried to instill into this website, and (as much as is possible) into the dozens and dozens of sites I've been a part of creating over the years.

I see this website as an extension of myself. I hope you explore a little bit and find something you weren't expecting. Cheers!

Sometimes your job as a designer is to do the most creative thing you can with the most UN-creative of subject matter… This is not one of those projects. With major league branding work from the folks over at RARE design, I worked hard to create an experience that was as vibrant and unique as the hispanic culture of Salt Lake, itself. This website is a little Salt Lake, a lot beisbol, and ALL fiesta.

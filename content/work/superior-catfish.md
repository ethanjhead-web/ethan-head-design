Unbeknownst to me until this project, Mississippi is the leading producer of catfish in the nation. While I can't say I enjoy catfish per se, creating the website for Superior was deliciously fun. The people behind the production of this southern delicacy are passionate about their craft and inspired me to make something that, hopefully, is worthy of Mississippi's title as the catfish capital of the world.

Duling Hall is THE premier music venue in central Mississippi and a part of Jackson history. As one of the only places in Mississippi that has drawn some of my favorite artists over the years, it's also a place that means a lot to me personally. That's why I was beyond excited to have the opportunity to create something for them that was as special as the venue, itself.

A project with a long and winding history, Sweet Mae's represents the power of committing to and following through on a vision and direction. With the explicit goal of incorporating the long culinary history of Tiffany's (the owner) ancestry into the branding, the site sews together family heritage and confectionery excellence into a unified experience that exudes southern charm.
